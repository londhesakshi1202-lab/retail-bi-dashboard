Executive Decision Report & Capstone Presentation

## Project Title
Retail Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes retail business data using interactive dashboards and business intelligence techniques.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze revenue and profit performance
- Identify customer and product trends
- Monitor key business KPIs
- Identify potential business risks
- Support data-driven decision making

## Key KPIs
- Revenue
- Customer Acquisition Cost
- Churn Rate
- Average Order Value
- Profit Performance

## Key Risk Areas
- Customer churn
- Low-performing products
- Regional performance gaps
- Changes in sales trends

## Strategic Recommendations
1. Improve customer retention through targeted engagement.
2. Focus on high-performing products and profitable segments.
3. Monitor regional and time-based trends to improve business decisions.
